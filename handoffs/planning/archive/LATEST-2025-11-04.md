## Context for Next Instance

### What Was Accomplished

This session completed the Q2 Meal Planning specification - the core value proposition of WeightGPT. This is where users spend most of their time in the app, viewing and interacting with their personalized weekly meal plans.

### Q2 Meal Planning Overview

**Scope:** Meal plan viewing, recipe details, meal swapping, feedback collection, shopping list generation, and weekly regeneration.

**Key Design Choices:**

1. **Daily Detail View** - Users swipe between days rather than viewing a calendar grid. This focuses attention on one day at a time and provides better mobile UX. Each day shows 3 meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) with full details.

2. **1-for-1 Meal Swapping** - When users don't like a meal, they can swap it for one of 3 alternatives. The alternatives match macros (±50 cal, ±5g protein) and respect all dietary preferences. Only that specific meal changes; other meals in the day stay the same.

3. **Learning Feedback System** - Users can rate meals with thumbs up/down. Thumbs down opens a detail screen to specify reasons (taste, time, cost, ingredients). This feedback influences future meal plan generation.

4. **Smart Shopping List** - Auto-generates after users review their weekly meals. Ingredients are consolidated with quantities (e.g., "4 chicken breasts, 2 lbs spinach"). Users can check items off while shopping, add custom items, or remove items they already have. Can share via text/email.

5. **Prompted Weekly Regeneration** - On the user's shopping day (from onboarding), they're prompted to generate next week's plan. They can choose to include favorite meals from previous weeks. This isn't automatic because users might miss their shopping day or shop early.

**Technical Approach:**
- Meal plans generate in background on first app open after subscription (15-20 seconds)
- All generation uses OpenAI GPT-4 with user preferences from Q1
- 9 data inputs drive generation: calories, macros, dietary preference, avoided foods, cuisines, prep time, variety, budget, shopping day
- Feedback aggregates over time to identify patterns and improve future plans

### Q1 Refinement: Cooking Context Removed

During Q2 planning, we identified that Q1's "cooking context" field ("How do you approach meals?" with options: cook at home / eat out often / mix of both) was not actionable.

**The insight:** Users of a meal planning app are inherently cooking at home. If they eat out frequently, they wouldn't download or use this app in the first place.

**The change:** Removed the field entirely. Q1 Step 9 now only asks about "Meal Prep Time" (minimal/moderate/extended). This reduces friction without losing any personalization value - the other fields (dietary preferences, cuisines, prep time) provide sufficient data.

**Q1 updated to v2.1** with this change reflected and documented.
